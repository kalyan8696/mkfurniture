About M K FURNITURE

We craft high-quality wooden furniture — where traditional woodworking meets modern design.

Our Story

M K FURNITURE began as a small workshop with a simple promise: build furniture that lasts. We source sustainable timber and handcraft each piece with careful joinery and refined finishes. Our focus is on durable construction, beautiful surfaces, and customer satisfaction.

Our Services

  • Custom furniture design
  • Home fittings & cabinetry
  • Restoration, refinishing & repairs

How We Work

We start with a conversation — measurements, material choices and finish options. After a design agreement, our craftsmen handcraft the piece, and we finish it to your chosen look. Delivery and installation can be arranged on request.

Name: Motilal sharma

Birth date: 01-05-1992

Success Story

Motilal began learning the craft in a small workshop and, over the years, turned his passion for woodworking into M K FURNITURE. Starting with a handful of local orders, he focused on durable construction and honest finishes. His reputation grew through word-of-mouth and custom commissions, allowing him to expand his workshop, train a small team of skilled carpenters, and deliver projects across Ahmedabad. Today, Motilal stays hands-on — mentoring apprentices, refining designs, and ensuring each piece meets his exacting standards.

Under his leadership, the workshop prioritizes sustainable timber sourcing and customer collaboration. Motilal's approach blends traditional joinery with modern finishes to create furniture that lasts generations.
